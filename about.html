About Us

We create custom, accessible websites for nonprofits and community organizations working to make a difference. By combining thoughtful design with functional development, we help organizations build an online presence that reflects who they are, communicates their mission, and reaches a wider audience.

Why We Exist

Small organizations can make a big impact, but don't always have the resources to build a website that represents their work. Cost, time, and technical barriers can stand between an organization and the people it is trying to reach. We exist to help close that gap by making quality web design more accessible, without sacrificing individuality or functionality.

Our Mission

To make thoughtful, custom web design accessible to organizations creating meaningful change.

We believe a limited budget shouldn't limit an organization's ability to be seen, understood, and supported online.
